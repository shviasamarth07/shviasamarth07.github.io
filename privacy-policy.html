ShotSearch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

ShotSearch ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android app that helps you find your screenshots by searching for the words and numbers inside them. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is developed by Samarth Mali.

1. The short version

  • Your screenshots and the text read from them stay on your phone.
  • We do not upload your screenshots or their text to our servers, and we do not have servers that store them.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers such as your advertising ID.
  • The app needs an internet connection to work.

2. Photos and screenshots

With your permission, ShotSearch reads the images in your device's Screenshots album. This is the main purpose of the app: it reads the text inside each screenshot using on-device text recognition, saves that text in a small database inside the app on your phone, and uses it so you can search quickly.

  • The images and the extracted text are stored only on your device.
  • We do not copy, upload, share, or sell your screenshots or the text found in them.
  • The app does not change or delete your screenshots.
  • You can remove the saved text at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Text recognition (Google ML Kit)

ShotSearch uses Google ML Kit text recognition. The text recognition itself runs on your device. ML Kit may send limited diagnostic and performance information (for example, error and usage statistics) to Google. This does not include the content of your screenshots. See ML Kit data disclosure.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of ShotSearch shows ads through Google AdMob (banner ads, full-screen ads, and optional rewarded video ads). To show ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Buying "Remove ads" removes ads from the app.

5. Purchases

ShotSearch offers an optional one-time purchase called "Remove ads". Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or bank details. The app saves on your device whether the purchase was made, and can restore it through your Google Play account.

6. Information stored on your device

The app also saves a few small settings on your device, such as:

  • The languages you selected for text recognition
  • Whether you purchased "Remove ads", and until when your free ad-free time lasts

7. Internet use

The app checks that your device is connected to the internet and needs a connection to work. The internet is used for ads and purchases. Your screenshots and their text are not sent over the internet by the app.

8. Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos / media (images): to find and read your screenshots, which is the main purpose of the app.
  • Internet and network state: to show ads, handle purchases, and check your connection.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

9. Children

ShotSearch is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

10. Data retention and deletion

Because your screenshot text is stored only on your device, you control it. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

11. Security

We keep your data on your device and do not collect it on our servers. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to limit what the app uses.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date at the top.

13. Contact us
